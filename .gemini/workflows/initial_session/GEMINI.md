# Initial Session Procedure

When starting a new chat session, follow these steps to quickly gain context on the project's overall goals and current progress:

1. **Review Project Scope**: Read `docs/PROJECT_SCOPE.md` to understand the high-level vision, technology stack, and long-term objectives of the project.
2. **Review Current Sprint**: Read `docs/sprints/current.md` to understand the immediate goals, completed tasks, and next steps for the ongoing sprint.
